![3D Sequencer workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces-18.png)

## How the work flows

3D Sequencer edits a film made of 3D shots. Each shot is a scene strip in the [Video Sequence Editor](../editors/video-sequence-editor/index.md), so you cut and reorder shots like clips. One [3D Viewport](../editors/3d-viewport/index.md) shows the camera of the current shot as the sequencer plays it, the other is where you stage and light that shot, and the [Dope Sheet](../editors/dope-sheet/index.md) holds its keys. The [Timeline](../editors/timeline/index.md) plays across all shots, and the [Outliner](../editors/outliner.md) and [Properties Editor](../editors/properties/index.md) keep scenes, cameras and output settings close.
