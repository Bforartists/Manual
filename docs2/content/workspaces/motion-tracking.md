A workspace for the VFX workflow. The Motion tracking layout.

![Motion Tracking workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces-15.png)

## How the work flows

Motion Tracking teaches the 3D camera to move like the real one. In the [Movie Clip Editor](../editors/movie-clip-editor/index.md) you place markers on details in the footage and track them frame by frame; two more views of the same editor show the tracks over time and their motion curves, so bad tracks stand out. Once the camera is solved, the [3D Viewport](../editors/3d-viewport/index.md) lets you check it against your scene, ready to place 3D objects into real footage. The [Timeline](../editors/timeline/index.md), [Outliner](../editors/outliner.md) and [Properties Editor](../editors/properties/index.md) cover frames, objects and camera settings.
