This layout is for general 3D work. Here you work at your 3D scene. Here happens modeling, texturing, etc. . It is also the workspace with which Bforartists opens up. It contains a 3D View editor, an Info editor, a Timeline editor, a Outliner editor and a Properties editor. It starts in Object Mode.

![Main workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces.png)

## Editors in this workspace

- [3D Viewport](../editors/3d-viewport/index.md)
- [Outliner](../editors/outliner.md)
- [Properties](../editors/properties/index.md)
- [Timeline](../editors/timeline/index.md)
- [Info Editor](../editors/info-editor.md)

## Starts in

The workspace opens the 3D Viewport in **Object Mode** when the scene has an object for it.
