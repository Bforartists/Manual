The UV Editing is made for texturing and UV mapping. It contains an Info Editor, a UV Editor and a 3D View editor. The 3D View starts in Edit mode.

![UV Editing workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces-2.png)

## How the work flows

UV Editing is where a model learns how to wear its texture. In the [3D Viewport](../editors/3d-viewport/index.md) you mark seams in Edit Mode, like the cut lines of a sewing pattern; unwrap, and the [UV Editor](../editors/uv-editor/index.md) lays those pieces flat over the image so you can move, scale and pack them. Every change shows on the model straight away, so you go back and forth between the two until the texture sits right. The [Outliner](../editors/outliner.md) picks the object, and the [Properties Editor](../editors/properties/index.md) holds its UV maps.

## Starts in

The workspace opens the 3D Viewport in **Edit Mode** when the scene has an object for it.
