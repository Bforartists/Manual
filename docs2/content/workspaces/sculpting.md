This workspace is dedicated to sculpting. It starts in Sculpt Mode in case you have an object in the scene.

![Sculpting workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces-9.png)

## How the work flows

Sculpting feels like working with digital clay. The [3D Viewport](../editors/3d-viewport/index.md) opens in Sculpt Mode with the brushes in the Tool Shelf and more in the Asset Shelf, so you can push, pull and smooth the form freely. When the clay runs out of detail, the [Properties Editor](../editors/properties/index.md) remeshes it or adds Multiresolution levels, and the [Outliner](../editors/outliner.md) switches between the pieces you are sculpting.

## Starts in

The workspace opens the 3D Viewport in **Sculpt Mode** when the scene has an object for it.
