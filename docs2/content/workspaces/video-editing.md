A workspace to edit videos.

![Video Editing workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces-16.png)

## How the work flows

Video Editing is a small cutting room. The [File Browser](../editors/file-browser.md) finds clips, images and sounds to drag in; the lower [Video Sequence Editor](../editors/video-sequence-editor/index.md) is the cutting table where you cut, move and fade strips on channels, and the upper one previews the edited picture at the current frame. The [Timeline](../editors/timeline/index.md) sets the range and plays it back, and the [Properties Editor](../editors/properties/index.md) chooses the output format, resolution and frame rate.
