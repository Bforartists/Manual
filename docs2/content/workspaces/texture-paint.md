The texture painting workspace is made to do texture painting work. It contains an Info Editor, a Image Editor and a 3D View editor. The 3D View starts in Edit mode.

![Texture Paint workspace](/docs2/images/workspaces/workspaces/standard-workspaces-6.png)

## Editors in this workspace

- [Image Editor](../editors/image-editor/index.md)
- [3D Viewport](../editors/3d-viewport/index.md)
- [Outliner](../editors/outliner.md)
- [Properties](../editors/properties/index.md)

## Starts in

The workspace opens the 3D Viewport in **Texture Paint Mode** when the scene has an object for it.
