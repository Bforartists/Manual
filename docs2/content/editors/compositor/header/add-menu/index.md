## Add menu

![Add menu](/docs2/images/editors/compositor/header/add-menu/add-menu.png)

Here you will find all the nodes that you need for compositing. A click at one of the items will create the node in the workspace at the mouse position right under the menu. It is already selected, and you can drag it around.

Note that the content may differ, dependent of which renderer you use. Some nodes just works with specific renderers. They will be marked as such.

Note also that some shaders are real resource hogs, and can slow down rendering times significantly.

## Add menu – Search...

![Add menu – Search...](/docs2/images/editors/compositor/header/add-menu/add-menu-search.png)

You can click on the search operator to bring up a Pop-up to search the Add menu where you can find specific node types in all categories.

To use, click on the operator or alternatively press spacebar or type in the term that you want to find.

![Add menu – Search...](/docs2/images/editors/compositor/header/add-menu/add-menu-search-2.png)

> **Note:** You can call the add menu then immediately start searching at any time.

Pick a category to see its nodes. Each category page lists its sub-menus in the panels on the right.
